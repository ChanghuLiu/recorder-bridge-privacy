Recorder Bridge Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Recorder Bridge helps users browse compatible USB storage and copy audio recordings to their Android device.

Audio recordings and files

When you select a folder using Android's system file picker, Recorder Bridge uses access to that folder to list and copy recordings you choose. Imported and converted copies are stored locally on your device. The app does not send recording audio to our servers as part of importing, playback, conversion, or local file management. The normal import flow copies files and does not intentionally rename or delete the source recording.

Google Play Billing diagnostics

The app uses Google Play Billing to show and process the one-time Lifetime Unlock, check purchase status, and acknowledge completed purchases. For this purpose, it reads the product ID and purchase status returned by Google Play and sends the purchase token back to Google Play to acknowledge a completed purchase. The app stores an entitlement flag locally. The app does not access your card or bank-account number; Google Play handles checkout and payment details.

The integrated Google Play Billing Library (version 9.1.0) includes automatic technical logging. During billing operations, its BillingLogger sends diagnostic events to Google using an encrypted HTTPS connection. The SDK code constructs billing-operation and result/error diagnostics, timing information, and technical app/device context such as the app build, Android version, device model and manufacturer, system build, language/region, network type, mobile-carrier code, and time-zone offset. The app does not receive the logger's server-side records. Google handles data received by its services under Google's terms and privacy practices; the app does not control Google's retention period or deletion process.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

No account or login is required. The app contains no advertising SDK. The Billing Library's technical logging described above is used for billing diagnostics; the app does not use a separate analytics service to profile users or their recordings.

Sharing and export

If you choose Share or Export, Android passes the selected recording copy to the destination app you choose. That app handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Google receives the billing diagnostics described above and processes purchase information needed for Google Play Billing.

Data retention and deletion

Recordings and app settings remain on your device unless you remove them. Recorder Bridge has no account system or developer-operated server where your recordings are stored. Billing diagnostic records sent to Google are handled by Google; refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices and controls.
