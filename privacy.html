Recorder Bridge Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Recorder Bridge is designed to help users access, import, and manage audio recordings from compatible USB voice recorders on Android devices.

Data Collection

Recorder Bridge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Audio Recordings

Audio recordings are processed locally on the user's device. Recorder Bridge does not upload recordings to any server.

USB Storage Access

Recorder Bridge accesses USB folders only after the user explicitly selects and grants access to a folder through Android's system file picker.

Accounts

No account or login is required.

Advertising and Analytics

Recorder Bridge does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Internet Access

The current version does not require a backend service for processing recordings.

Data Sharing

Recorder Bridge does not share user data with third parties.
